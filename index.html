A Letter from the Commander

Hello Legionaries,

The time for a bingo break is here. Our building is moving forward with a new HVAC, and the Star conference is around the corner. I’ll be representing our post and District 2 this year. We will be voting on a new Commander for AL State. Al Jensen is the only one I’ve seen a resolution from.
I’ll be picking up the new membership cards as well. So don’t for get to see Rich to get your new card after I come back.
I want to send a Bravo Zulu out to Lynn and Sue for the work they have done for Post 91 and supporting veterans. There’s no way we could have done it without them leading in different ways. Thank for being the best of us.
Veterans Day is not to far away. If you’d like to be a part of the festivities let me know. Bob Johnson is heading up a fun run/walk fundraiser before the Veterans Day Parade and I’m on the parade committee. Thank you to the team who makes our building better and safer. You are making a difference.
Happy 250th!!!!

Respectfully,
Chris Diaz
Commander
